Quick answers

The mic isn't reacting

Make sure MIC is on and that Pocket VJ Pro is allowed to use the microphone in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. Then raise Sensitivity. In a very loud room, lower it so the picture doesn't stay maxed out.

No picture on the TV

With a cable: check that the adapter and HDMI cable are pushed all the way in and the TV is on the right input. If it still shows nothing, try another cable or adapter.

With AirPlay: keep your device and the TV on the same Wi-Fi, then pick the TV from Screen Mirroring in Control Center.

Restore purchases

If you bought a plan and it isn't showing, open the plans screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
